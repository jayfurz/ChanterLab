Finding the pthora

The machinery is built: a fthora sets the note's degree and switches the genus, exactly as the atlas states it — "the note takes on the new degree quality in the new scale". What I cannot tell is which printed combination signals one, and I would rather ask than guess.

Every scale sign in the book is either stacked with a martyria letter (5995) or alone on a heading line with no neumes (344). None sits over a note by itself, so the atlas's "over a melodic glyph" shape does not occur here in isolation.

But your anatomy — "the greek letters on the top portion are the parallagi syllable, the symbols below indicate degree and scale" — shows up in the data. The two commonest stacks name the same degree twice (Βου over Βου ×377, Δι over Δι ×150), which is letter-plus-indicator agreeing. About 55 stacks disagree, and your p375 example is one of them — Πα on top, then Κε with the ananes sign under it. Those are my pthora candidates.
