Cluster 7: psifiston, or psifiston and omalon? — 175 odd ones out

You said about half the kentimata figures had omalon underneath. On the atlas that should have been impossible — omalon is cluster 36 and it does not occur in those figures at all. But cluster 7's glyph height is bimodal with an empty gap: 7295 instances at 6.2–6.4 pt, 175 at 6.7–6.9 pt, and nothing at 6.5 or 6.6. Rendered, the common one is a deep U-swoop and the rare one a flat level stroke. Inside the 78 you ruled, the flat ones take an argon on top (37 of 38) and the swooped ones a gorgon (40 of 40).

Neither carries an interval, so no parallagi degree moves either way. What it changes is the atlas and the beat model — omalon ties two notes and carries no beat, and a mis-set omalon has already been caught adding a spurious beat elsewhere.
